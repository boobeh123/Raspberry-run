Raspberry Run

Keep the CPU cool. Don't get throttled.

How to play

  • Space, ↑, or tap to jump. Hold for a higher jump.
  • P or Esc to pause.
  • Jump over the hot CPU chips.
  • The CPU heats up as you run, and the game speeds up with it.
  • Grab snowflakes to cool down. Hit 85 °C and you're thermal throttled.

Made on a Raspberry Pi 5
